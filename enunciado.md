Super Mario, also known as Super Mario Bros or simply Mario, is a platform game series
developed and published by Nintendo, featuring their iconic character, Mario. The series
was created by Japanese game designer Shigeru Miyamoto and serves as the flagship series
of the broader Mario franchise. At least one Super Mario game has been released for every
major Nintendo video game console, and a limited number of titles have also been made
available on non-Nintendo gaming platforms. Overall, there are more than 20 games in the
series. For more information, the reader is referred to https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/
Super_Mario and https://github.com/Bennyhwanggggg/Super-Mario-Clone-Cpp.

Guidelines
In the construction of this game, you need to aware of the following:
• Illumination, materials, and shading. This is important to create realism and to
take most of 3D Super Mario game.
• Texturing. Texturing is also important to foster scene realism.
• Interaction. Handling your character in its run and gathering of points and badges
using input devices like a mouse or keys is a must in this project.
• Special effects and add-ons. Special effects are not required in this game. But
doing that leads to extra marks in your grade.
• Novelty. Novelty in designing, developing, and coding this game is awarded with
extra marks in your grade.
In case a student presents a 2D version (rather than a 3D one) of a car racing game, a
cut of 2 out of 10 marks will affect your final grade. The entire code of the project must
be written using Modern OpenGL, GLM, GLFW, and GLEW (ou GLAD).
